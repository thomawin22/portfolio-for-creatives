  • About me!

    I’m a interaction design student based in North Vancouver. I have a background in Graphic Design including a Diploma from VCAD (Visual College of Art and Design). I value a composite of creative, user-tailored, clear and intuitive designs!

    Growing up, I often turned to storytelling and creative projects as a way to express ideas, which naturally evolved into a passion for design. After completing my graphic design diploma, I chose to continue my education in Capilano University’s IDEA program, where I’ve found a renewed sense of purpose and collaboration. I’m passionate about creating thoughtful, engaging digital experiences and continuously growing as both a designer and problem solver.
