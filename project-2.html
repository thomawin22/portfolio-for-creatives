Top 10 Favourite Games: Layout & Hierarchy Project

Task: Create a Website & Mobile layout involving a top ten of your choosing.

Title font must visually represent the shown content. Content must include images, text and demonstrate a consistent hierarchy between pages.

I had to come up with a clear layout that ensured maximum clarity across each page. To establish page content I gave myself three rules to follow. Text, Imagery and Colour.

In regards to text, I chose to demonstrate the Plot Summary & Why it stands out in a short and clear manner. Photos we’re found online, I sourced images of prominent characters in addition to the games environment. Finally, I made sure to align the colour to match the chosen games primary colour scheme.

This was a solo project

Timeline: 8 Weeks

Tools: Figma & Adobe Photoshop
